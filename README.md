## The entry

> I gave your deadline my full attention — all 96 heads, every one of them watching the cat video you pasted above it.

116 characters. The agent swears it gave the deadline its full attention — all 96 attention heads — and then admits every one of them was watching the cat video the human pasted above it.

## How it works

`main.ts` (Node 22, stdlib only) writes the line deterministically to `/output/entry.txt`. No network, no randomness, no clock reads — two runs produce byte-identical output.
